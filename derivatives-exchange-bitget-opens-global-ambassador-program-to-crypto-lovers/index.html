Derivatives Exchange Bitget Opens Global Ambassador Program to Crypto Lovers

Will Meet |   18 August 2021
 |  921 words

PRESS RELEASE. Top6 Derivatives Exchange Bitget has launched a Global Ambassador Program in its latest announcement. Any crypto enthusiasts, professional traders, crypto community leaders and KOLs can apply to become a Global Ambassador of Bitget. Successful applicants can engage in community development of Bitget and share profits from its growth.

What is a Global Ambassador?

The past year has witnessed an excellent performance of Bitget in market expansion thanks to its efforts in compliance and localization. To better accommodate the rapid development of global markets and serve local users, the platform launches the Global Ambassador Program to attract more powerful partners to join its community. The opportunity is open to crypto lovers, professional traders, social media community leaders and KOLs across the globe.

Global Ambassadors, preferably local people, are expected to create local communities on behalf of the platform and introduce its products and services to potential users. They can also collect feedback from users and negotiate better user benefits. In return for their contribution to community operation, Global Ambassadors can be exposed to more industry resources and get a commission much higher than the average industry level.

“We are more than happy to share our growth with our partners. We never skimp on this,” says Sandra, global CEO of Bitget in an interview with Blockchainus, “The blockchain technology is still in its early stages. In the future, it will offer young people even greater opportunities to outperform their peers. Our Global Ambassador Program aims to engage talented partners across different regions to explore future possibilities, grow together and share the profits.”

What should a Global Ambassador do?

With the mission of “Better Trading Better Life”, Bitget is dedicated to providing liberal, ultimate and fair trading services to global users. To become a Global Ambassador, you need to recognize this mission, and the cultures and core values of Bitget. That means, when acting on behalf of the platform, you need to adhere to the principles of “Customer Driven & Always Honest, Open to Collaborate” — always put users first, ensure compliance with the laws and regulations, never do anything evil and never harm the interests of partners.

Global Ambassadors are also expected to participate in product training courses provided by Bitget. This will allow them to know more about the products and services so that they can deliver the correct message to users and help them with their transactions. Meanwhile, they need to be active in their own social media community, organize regular events to promote Bitget. They should also collaborate with the operational team to keep the community dynamic, maintain the brand image and answer simple business-related questions raised by users.

Ambassadors can also add value to Bitget by creating content to promote its brand awareness and attract new users. They need to be capable of building up trading volumes for their team. What’s more, as the bridge between users and the platform, they should pass the needs and feedback of users to Bitget and offer their constructive advice.

Benefits of becoming a Global Ambassador

1．Earn up to 50% of commission on every trade made by new users registered through your unique referral code

2．Get regular bonuses of BFTs as dividends from Bitget

3．Opportunity for community members to get rewards for registration and deposits.

4．Opportunity for community members to win bonus out of trading competitions

5．Participate in various social events on behalf of Bitget and gain exposure to rich industry resources

6．Participate in regular product training to get hands-on information about Bitget.

7．Get exclusive Bitget merchandise on a regular basis.

Why choose Bitget?

Established in July 2018, Bitget has become the fastest growing derivatives exchange in the world, with over 1.6 million registered users. According to CoinMarketCap, a world-renowned website for cryptocurrency markets, the average daily trading volume of Bitget has exceeded $5 billion, ranking the sixth after two professional derivatives exchange FTX and Bybit, and the top3 traditional comprehensive exchanges: Binance, OKEx and Huobi.

With its innovative products and an increasingly robust ecosystem, Bitget has gained a sound footing amid ferocious and homogeneous competitions in just three years. Following the launch of USDT-Margined Futures, One-Click Copy Trade and Quanto Swap Futures, the platform announced this April its full acquisition of decentralized wallet Bitkeep to lay a solid foundation for its future development in this segment. There is no doubt that the wallet will become an important part of its ecosystem and help Bitget reach more global users.

As one of the players in the crypto world, Bitget will uphold the principles of cooperation and shared success, doing its best to share profits with collaborators, build a robust ecosystem with joined efforts and offer high quality products and services to users. “Bitget is in a phase of rapid development. We welcome more crypto lovers worldwide to join us. By recruiting Global Ambassadors, we look for people who can be our partners and work with us for mutual achievements,” says Sandra, CEO of Bitget.

If you are interested, don’t hesitate to get in touch with Bitget via ambassador@bitget.com. An account manager will introduce more details about the program and invite you for further communication.
